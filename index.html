About

Design built to work as hard as it looks.

I run Javel Materials and Services Inc. and take on freelance, project-based work as a graphic designer — building the brand identities, brochures, ID systems, signage, and social assets that companies actually put in front of customers.

Every piece I design is judged the same way: does it read clearly at a glance, and does it stay consistent everywhere it shows up — from a trifold brochure to a booth banner to an ID card. I’ve designed for telecom brands serving thousands of active users, food & beverage startups building a look from zero, and corporate print ads that had to land in one page.
